Atlas Metals Bullion Desk - Trade Confirmation Rules (synthetic training data)

Standard confirmations
- Every executed spot or forward trade generates a trade confirmation for the client.
- Confirmations use the standard confirmation format unless the client has an approved custom template.
- Quantities are always shown in troy ounces and prices in the trade currency per troy ounce.

Client-specific templates
- A client may request a custom confirmation template with their own layout and wording.
- Every custom template needs legal sign-off on the wording before it can be used in production.
- A custom template is built and tested locally, then released to UAT, then signed off by the business and by legal.
- Template changes after sign-off restart the approval process, even for a one-word change.

Timing and corrections
- Confirmations must be sent within one hour of trade capture.
- A trade that is cancelled or amended after confirmation requires a corrected confirmation that references the original.
- Rounding: quantities are shown to three decimal places and prices to two decimal places.

Matching
- The client's counter-confirmation is matched to the trade on trade id, metal, quantity, price and settlement date.
- An unmatched confirmation after the next business day is escalated to the operations lead.

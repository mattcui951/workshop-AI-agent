Atlas Metals Bullion Desk - Settlement and Cutoff Policy (synthetic training data)

Settlement cycles
- Spot gold and spot silver settle two business days after the trade date (T+2).
- Spot platinum and spot palladium also settle on T+2 unless the client's agreement says otherwise.
- Forward trades settle on the agreed settlement date, which must be a business day in both markets.

Same-day settlement
- Same-day settlement instructions must be received before 13:00 New York time.
- Instructions received after the cutoff are processed on the next available settlement day.
- Same-day settlement is only available for trades fully confirmed by the client.

Failed settlements
- A failed settlement must be reported to the settlement operations desk within one hour of detection.
- Repeated failures with the same counterparty are escalated to the counterparty risk team.

Sanctions and payment stops
- When a sanction is applied to a counterparty, new payments to that counterparty must stop immediately.
- Payments already in flight are reviewed by compliance, who decide whether each one is held or released.
- Any statistics shared with the business about a sanctioned counterparty must go through the compliance process.

Atlas Metals Trading Database - Table Reference (synthetic training data)

TABLE trade
- trade_id: unique id of the trade, text, for example T-100234.
- metal_code: AU for gold, AG for silver, PT for platinum, PD for palladium.
- quantity_oz: quantity in troy ounces. Kilo bars are converted at 32.1507 troy ounces per kilogram.
- price_per_oz: agreed price per troy ounce in the trade currency.
- currency: three letter currency code of the price.
- counterparty_id: links to the counterparty table.
- trade_date: the date the trade was executed.
- settlement_date: the date cash and metal are exchanged.
- status: settlement status of the trade. Values are NEW, CONFIRMED, SETTLED, FAILED and CANCELLED.

TABLE counterparty
- counterparty_id: unique id of the client or bank.
- name: legal name of the counterparty.
- risk_tier: internal risk grade from 1 (lowest risk) to 5 (highest risk).
- sanction_flag: Y when a sanction applies to the counterparty, otherwise N.

TABLE inventory_position
- vault_id: the vault holding the metal.
- metal_code: same codes as the trade table.
- quantity_oz: metal held in troy ounces at the end of the as_of_date.
- as_of_date: the business date of the position snapshot.

Query tips
- To find all trades for a counterparty, join trade to counterparty on counterparty_id.
- Cancelled trades stay in the trade table with status CANCELLED, so filter them out for volume statistics.
- The inventory_position table holds one row per vault, metal and as_of_date.

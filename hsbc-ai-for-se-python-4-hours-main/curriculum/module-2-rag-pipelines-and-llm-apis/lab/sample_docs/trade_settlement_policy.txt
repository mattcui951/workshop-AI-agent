Atlas Markets — Trade Settlement Policy (synthetic training data)

Standard settlement cycle:
- Equities settle on a T+1 basis (trade date plus one business day).
- Fixed income instruments settle on a T+1 or T+2 basis depending on
  instrument type; confirm against the instrument's settlement
  instructions before assuming T+1.
- FX spot transactions settle on a T+2 basis.

Settlement cutoff times:
- Same-day settlement instructions must be submitted before 2:00 PM
  local market time.
- Instructions submitted after the cutoff are processed on the next
  available settlement cycle.

Failed settlement handling:
- A failed settlement must be flagged to the settlement operations
  desk within one hour of the failure being detected.
- Repeated failures on the same counterparty must be escalated to
  the counterparty risk team for review.

Atlas Markets — Payments Reconciliation Guide (synthetic training data)

Daily reconciliation process:
1. Pull the prior day's completed payment events from the payment
   rail's event log.
2. Match each event against the internal ledger by payment ID.
3. Flag any payment present in one system but not the other as a
   reconciliation break.

Handling reconciliation breaks:
- Breaks under a configured materiality threshold are logged and
  reviewed at the next daily standup.
- Breaks over the materiality threshold require same-day escalation
  to the reconciliation lead and must be resolved or explained within
  24 hours.

Data handling:
- Reconciliation reports must not include full account numbers —
  use the last four digits only.
- Reports are retained for 7 years per record-retention policy.

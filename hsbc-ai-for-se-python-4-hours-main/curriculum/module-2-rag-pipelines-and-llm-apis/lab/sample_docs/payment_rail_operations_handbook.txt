Atlas Markets - Payment Rail Operations Handbook (synthetic training data)

Payment states:
- submitted: the payment has been sent to the rail and is waiting for confirmation.
- settled: the rail has confirmed that funds moved. Settled is final.
- failed: the rail rejected the payment or it timed out. Failed is final and the payment must be re-submitted as a new payment.

Notifications from the rail:
- The rail sends a notification every time a payment changes state.
- If our service does not answer a notification with a success code, the rail retries up to five times over two hours.
- The same notification can therefore arrive more than once, and notifications can arrive out of order.

Support and escalation:
- A payment still submitted after four hours is treated as stuck and raised with the rail's support desk.
- Stuck payments over the high-value limit are escalated to the duty manager at once.
- Every escalation records the payment id, the amount, the time submitted and who raised it.

Maintenance windows:
- The rail is unavailable for maintenance on the first Sunday of each month from 01:00 to 03:00 UTC.
- Payments submitted during a maintenance window are held and sent when the rail returns.

# Prompt: judge a draft answer as the reviewer

You are helping me review a draft answer from a document-grounded assistant. Attached are the question, the draft answer and the
numbered source chunks it was given.

For every factual claim in the draft, tell me whether it appears in a source, and which one. Flag any claim that does not.
Flag any number, name or time that is not in the sources. End with a one-line verdict: approve, or reject and why.
